## A - CDs 60-62 - Typed Transcripts.pdf - blocked

Page 1 is a project-wide transcription tracking sheet. Pages 2-27 are Welsh District material. Pages 31-38 are Pontypool material, pages 39-126 are Swansea material, and pages 127-176 are Pontypool/Abersychan material.

The boundary is not clean: pages 28-30 are marked by the original project as duplicated/mixed material from CDs 60 and 62. PDF page 30 contains Welsh District text in its upper portion and begins Pontypool text in its lower portion. A page-level lossless split cannot separate those two record groups without either duplicating the mixed page or altering its content.

Provisional, non-approved ranges are therefore:

| Pages | Record group | Status |
|---:|---|---|
| 2-27 | Welsh District, 1849-1854 | Clear range, but held with the source PDF |
| 28-30 | Mixed Welsh District and Pontypool | **Ambiguous - no split proposed** |
| 31-38 | Pontypool, 1857-1858 | Clear range, but held with the source PDF |
| 39-126 | Swansea General Minutes, 1852-1854 | Clear range, but held with the source PDF |
| 127-176 | Pontypool/Abersychan General Minutes, 1857-1889 | Clear range, but held with the source PDF |

No derived PDF from this source should be produced or uploaded until the mixed pages are resolved explicitly.

## Approval gate

After approval of these mappings and a decision for `A - CDs 60-62 - Typed Transcripts.pdf`, the next phase will create and visually verify the lossless PDFs, record source provenance in `INDEX.csv`, refresh Archive.org metadata and abort on any collision, upload the approved files, and rerun full checksum reconciliation. The Viewer source remains unchanged until that audit reports zero missing intended images and zero checksum conflicts.
