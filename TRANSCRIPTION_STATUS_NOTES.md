## Interpretation rule

`Complete`, `transcribed`, `not done`, `Welsh`, and page-range statements should be stored as source assertions dated 2007. A PDF containing typed pages is evidence that those pages survive, but it is not evidence that every page of the corresponding original volume was transcribed.
