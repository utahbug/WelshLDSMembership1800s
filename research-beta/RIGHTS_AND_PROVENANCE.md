# Rights, permission, and provenance notes

This file records information supplied by the project custodian so future maintainers understand where the material came from and what permissions have been reported. It is a provenance record, not a legal opinion.

## Ron Dennis material

- **Author and source:** Ron Dennis provided the project custodian with material used in these Welsh historical projects.
- **Reported permission:** The project custodian states that Ron Dennis gave permission to use his material in any manner the custodian considered appropriate.
- **Books:** The project custodian reports that most of Ron Dennis's books have been converted to PDF, are no longer sold, and that Deseret Book returned the rights to him.
- **Planned treatment:** Ron Dennis's books are a future project phase. They should not be added until the LDS record archive and its handoff documentation are stable.
- **Attribution:** Material known to be authored or supplied by Ron Dennis should retain his name, original title, publication details when known, and any source notes accompanying the files.

If an email, letter, or other written record of permission is located later, preserve a copy outside the public repository when it contains private information and add a brief reference here.

## Student transcriptions and translations

Student work should retain the contributor's name when known. Do not silently replace a human transcription or translation with AI-generated text. Record whether text is human-produced, AI-assisted, AI-produced, or reviewed by a named person.

## Record images and institutional notices

Permission concerning Ron Dennis's authored material does not automatically determine the rights status of record images obtained from Church, library, archival, or other institutional sources. Preserve any copyright, call-number, ownership, or use notices found with the original files. Public distribution decisions should be made separately from local preservation and cataloging.

## Preservation rule

Never remove attribution, copyright notices, call numbers, provenance notes, or source filenames merely to simplify the viewer. Standardized catalog labels may be added, but the original evidence should remain available.
